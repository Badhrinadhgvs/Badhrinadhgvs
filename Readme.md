## About

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at Narayana Engineering College, Nellore, graduating in 2027 with a 9.3/10 CGPA. My work sits at the intersection of **applied AI/ML** and **enterprise platform engineering** — I've shipped adaptive assessment systems, agentic AI tools, and ServiceNow governance workflows, and I build full-stack products end to end.

In 2025, I led a team to a national hackathon win against 170 competing teams. I hold ServiceNow's CSA and CAD certifications, and I'm currently deep in campus placement preparation while building **SEPMS**, a production-grade employee and project management platform.
